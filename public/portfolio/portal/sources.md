## Project facts

The case identifies Rico as Product Manager over nine months, with five engineers and one designer, leading the lifecycle from research and development through go-to-market. Four delivery phases cover central asset management and synchronization; partner capabilities including evidence, integrations, requests, sharing and monetization; migration and communications; and guidance, support and instrumentation. Contribution copy describes leadership and coordination, without attributing every design or engineering task to Rico.

The original connection diagram names EdCo Marketplace, Canvas, Impact and LearnPlatform. Its adjacent prose references five interconnected products. This treatment reproduces the four named destinations and does not invent a fifth or assert data-flow direction.

The project owner additionally described paid services, including ESSA evaluations, as part of the partner program's revenue model. Neither the case nor this treatment supplies a revenue amount, conversion rate or attributed revenue growth.

The graph names the account series Monthly Active Accounts (MAA); surrounding prose also calls it total accounts. This treatment preserves the graph label. The source does not establish a consistent account definition or calculation, so the percentages remain source-reported values. The graph shares a zero label at launch, used for both series. The case also reports eight new features among the application's most-used; no independent ranking or quantified adoption per feature is supplied.
