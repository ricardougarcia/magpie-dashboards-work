# Heron: attention and discovery

Observed September 12, 2026 at 1440 × 1000 in Chrome. Source: [Heron](https://heronaiapp.com/), a concept site credited to Bearplus. These are read-only reference captures, not Portfolio designs.

| Observed state | Capture | Portfolio interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A dominant architectural drawing sits between open margins and a lower title band. | [Resting hero](reference/01-hero-rest.jpg) | Let the original work lead; place its explanation second. |
| Dragging draws a selection rectangle and exposes anchored violation notes. Releasing removes the tint while notes remain. | [Drag inspection](reference/02-hero-drag-active.jpg) | Make direct interaction reveal a useful, local fact. |
| The following section changes scale and composition while retaining paper and line language. | [Composition change](reference/04-scroll-transition.jpg) | Change narrative density without changing identity. |
| A framed scene pauses on one centered sentence. | [Narrative pause](reference/05-map-entry.jpg) | Negative space can establish a new focal point. |
| Continued scrolling introduces floor-plan layers and inspection targets in a stable field. | [Layered plan](reference/08-map-violation-targets.jpg) | Preserve orientation while evidence becomes more detailed. |
| Selecting a marker opens nearby detail while keeping the plan visible. | [Local explanation](reference/09-map-open-violation.jpg) | Expand evidence in context rather than scattering disclosure buttons. |

Exact durations/easing were not measured. Mobile, keyboard, reduced motion, and performance were not evaluated. Heron uses textured paper and perimeter rulers; a uniform graph grid was not established. Its repeated arrow links and long pinned scene are not recommendations. The Portfolio translation is a design proposal; no Heron product claims transfer to Marketplace.

Images are scaled JPEG exports of unaltered browser captures. Full captures and inspection scripts are retained in the local verification archive. The Portfolio storyboard uses original Marketplace images, not generated facsimiles.
