## Composition and motion

The composition is compact and unboxed: three outcomes on the first desktop row and two on the second. Each outcome has a prominent final figure, a short label, and one supporting line. All five have a clear reading order and remain part of the document. Phone layouts stack the outcomes without horizontal overflow. Content can wrap and the section remains in normal document flow; viewport height is not enforced by clipping content.

On first entry, each final figure reveals through a short mask and settles upward by 6px over 420ms. The stagger is 80ms, with `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`. Labels and supporting lines remain stationary. Each figure plays once per page visit, then stays in its completed state. The figures are never counted up or substituted with temporary values. There is no pinning, looping, hover requirement, or scroll-controlled replay.

All five outcomes and their complete text are server-rendered and readable without JavaScript. Motion is progressive enhancement. Reduced motion shows the completed composition immediately. The disclosure uses native accessible semantics, retains keyboard operation and visible focus, and requires no hover to open. The Board return remains directly accessible.
