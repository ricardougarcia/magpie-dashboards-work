## Interaction and accessibility

When the opening fits, the masthead and entire opening composition remain held while native scrolling advances Emerging AI Marketplace, Edu App Center, LearnCommunity Library, then the shared Marketplace. The image, selected source, and caption follow one reversible timeline. After the final hold, the heading word “catalogs” travels into the section navigation as the opening fades and Research replaces it. The navigation first appears during this handoff, without a top or bottom rule, and marks Research current. The same Research section then introduces the lens; there is no duplicate preview article.

Source choices and Continue to Research provide direct alternatives. Image activation opens the original inspection with fit and actual-size views. All four catalog originals, captions, and the primary narrative are server-rendered. Native file links work without JavaScript; existing planning and measurement disclosures retain their qualifications. A measured fit check enables the compact catalog sequence on sufficiently tall phones. Short screens, narrow landscape, content that no longer fits, reduced motion, and catalog-artifact hashes use normal flow with all four images. Keyboard focus, direct hashes, history, resizing, and inspection have explicit continuity safeguards. Exact catalog progress intervals, geometry, input rules, and reference differences are recorded in the [Catalog Confluence contract](marketplace-confluence.md).

Research has an independent measured hold below the masthead and section navigation when the lens composition fits and its field is at least 640px wide. The heading remains in normal flow. Native scrolling shifts attention across three areas of one listing, then from educator to provider, before the shared conclusion appears. Perspective controls and the listing areas offer direct interaction. A visible caption identifies the listing as conceptual, not a specific product or certification. The annotations express research framing and are not presented as participant quotations. Without JavaScript, with reduced motion, or when the composition does not fit, all six needs and the conclusion remain available in normal flow. The live page excludes the study's Play, progress slider, layout selector, Tweak controls, and Research artifact disclosure. The [Research lens contract](marketplace-research-lens.md) records its timing, content, and fallback requirements. The route remains noindex/nofollow.
